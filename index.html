    Gift Note

    Your Presence is the Greatest Gift

    Your presence is the greatest gift to us. If you wish to bless us further, a monetary gift would be sincerely appreciated, as we will be returning to Canada after our wedding and may not be able to bring traditional gifts with us. Your generosity will help us as we begin again together. Thank you for the love and support!

    Little Ones

    Children Policy

    Please keep an eye on your little ones throughout the celebration. During the ceremony, kindly ensure they remain seated and avoid running, shouting, or disturbing the proceedings. We appreciate your cooperation in making our special day comfortable and enjoyable for everyone.
